Health states and the recovery path

Three health states, two timers, one recovery path. Before LOCKED, the status message reports FAILED / initializing; the HealthMonitor itself starts OK.

Timers: 0.6 s scan, 1.0 s GNSS, 2.0 s recovery / failure / sources-stale, 0.6 s scan-stale. FAILED never returns directly to OK.
