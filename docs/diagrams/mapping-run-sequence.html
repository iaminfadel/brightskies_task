Inside one live mapping run

Teleport first, then record. The route driver owns the 10 Hz control loop; the bridge publishes sensors, transforms, and sim time; the bag captures six topics until the driver stops.

Six recorded topics: /carla/hero/lidar, /carla/hero/gnss, /carla/hero/imu, /tf, /tf_static, /clock. The bag closes 2 s after the driver returns.
